## (c) Operator first-touch email — base template

```
Subject: 20 minutes on a completion tracker for [Trail Name]?

Hi [Contact Name],

I'm Matt [Last Name], building GolfRaven — an app that lets golfers track which courses they've
played on a trail, see their progress, and (with the operator's sign-off) earn a numbered finisher's
marker collected in person at a member pro shop. I'm reaching out to golf trails directly during a
short research phase before I build anything trail-specific.

For [Trail Name] specifically, this would mean:
- listing and a completion tracker at zero integration cost to you or your courses — no POS or
  tee-sheet changes;
- pro-shop staff tap "Marker sold" (or read out a daily code) when a golfer buys any ball marker
  already on your rack — that's the only new staff action;
- a numbered special marker that [Trail Name] orders and stocks at its own shops for finishers to
  collect in person — nothing is shipped by us, ever;
- a per-season programme fee that covers the tracking, marker programme and reporting tooling.

I'd like 20 minutes on a call to hear whether this fits, what you'd need to see before committing to
a pilot season, and who else should be on the call. No commitment either way — just an exploratory
conversation.

Would [DAY/TIME OPTION 1] or [DAY/TIME OPTION 2] work?

Thanks,
Matt [Last Name]
[Entity name — TBD]
[Phone]
[Email]
```

### Per-trail variant paragraphs (insert in place of the generic bullet list above)

**Tennessee Golf Trail (public body — TN State Parks):**
```
I know public bodies have procurement rules — I'd follow whatever process your team requires. No
player data is sold or shared with any state agency beyond aggregate visit counts you'd see in your
own operator dashboard.
```

**Vancouver Island Golf Trail (references the existing digital Trail Pass):**
```
I understand Golf Vancouver Island already runs a digital Trail Pass, so I'm not proposing a
competing product — this would sit alongside it as a completion tracker and a collectible marker
programme layered on top of the courses golfers already play via the Pass. If it's easier to think of
this as "what happens after a golfer finishes the Trail Pass season," that's close to the idea.
```

**Robert Trent Jones Golf Trail (RSA — extends the Trail Card, does not compete with it):**
```
I want to be clear up front: this is not a competing card or a discount that undercuts the $49.95
Trail Card. The idea is to make the Trail Card's own benefits visible in-app — a golfer's progress
toward playing all 26 courses, and (if RSA wants it) a special finisher's marker as a Trail Card-holder
reward, checked at the counter alongside the Card itself. RSA would author every discount offer in the
app, so nothing undercuts Trail Card pricing unless RSA chooses it. Every link back to purchasing or
renewing the Trail Card stays pointed at rtjgolf.com.
```
