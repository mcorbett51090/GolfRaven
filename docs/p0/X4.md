## METHOD

**Exact URLs / pattern to fetch** (from `docs/golf-trails/research/golfnow-integration.md`):

- Deep-link pattern: `https://www.golfnow.com/tee-times/facility/<facility-id>-<slug>/search`
  (pattern directly observed live in the research session against three independent facility pages).
- Facility IDs are **not** derivable algorithmically — the research explicitly says to "manually look each
  RTJ/trail course up on golfnow.com once and hard-code the mapping." Already-observed RTJ facility IDs (a
  partial set, from the research file, not yet the full 11): `2360` Grand National, `2353` Oxmoor Valley,
  `2358` Hampton Cove, `2361` Cambrian Ridge.
- For TN (9 courses) and VI (11–13 courses), no facility IDs have been looked up yet — none are in the research
  files.

**Parsing rule:**

1. For each pilot-slate course (full rosters come from X2), search `golfnow.com` for the course name and
   record whether a live facility page exists at the `/tee-times/facility/<id>-<slug>/search` pattern (not a
   404, and not a redirect to a generic search with no results).
2. Record `facility-id`, `slug`, and `checkedAt` (date) for every hit — this is also the seed data for the
   plan's §6.1 T0 deep-link tier and the booking-host CI gate (§6.3).
3. Coverage % = (courses with a confirmed live facility page) / (total pilot-slate courses from X2's rosters).
4. Compute coverage **per trail** as well as combined, since the kill consequence ("course-native link becomes
   primary for *that trail*") is per-trail, not global.
