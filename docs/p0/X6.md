## VERDICT

**adjust**, against the bar "Full text obtained **and** no clause forbids linking to public facility pages".

*Interpretation, not legal advice. This read feeds counsel L4 and does not replace it.*

**Does any clause forbid linking to public facility pages?** No clause obtained says so in terms. E1 never
addresses inbound linking, and no clause prohibits a plain hyperlink. The nearest text is E11 "No Repurposing":
"make the Versant Services or any Content available via frames or in-line links". In ordinary web usage,
"in-line linking" means embedding another site's content (an image, a frame) inside your own page, as opposed to
a hyperlink the user clicks to leave. On that reading, the T0 mechanism falls outside it: a plain `<a href>` to
`https://www.golfnow.com/tee-times/facility/<id>-<slug>/search` that opens GolfNow's own page, with no framing,
no embedded GolfNow content and no GolfNow prices shown. This memo does not stretch the clause to cover that
link. It does not dismiss the clause either, because "in-line links" is undefined in the text.

**Why this is not `pass`:**

1. **"Full text obtained" is not cleanly met.** §5.1, the ToS's only operative list of restrictions, incorporates
   "this list of Prohibited Actions" by a hyperlink that returns **404** (E9, E10). The substitute text (E11) is
   an inference about what was incorporated, not an observation of it. The Help Center/FAQs (Supplemental Terms
   under §1.3) were not read.
2. **Ambiguity that bears on T0**, which counsel should read:
   - E11 "No Repurposing": "in-line links" (undefined).
   - §2.1: "personal, non-commercial use only", together with E11 "No Commercial Use": "make any other commercial
     use of … any portion of the Versant Services". GolfRaven is a commercial product that points golfers at
     GolfNow pages, so it is unclear whether *referring* traffic counts as "use of" the Services.
   - E11 "No Impersonation": "use the Versant Services in a manner that suggests an association with our …
     brands". This one concerns labelling (see below).

**Why this is not `kill`:** no clause forbids deep links in terms, and the ToS text *was* obtained. On the
day-5 limb: *inference*, taking day 0 as the memo's creation on 2026-09-23, day 5 falls around 2026-09-28 and
has not passed.

**Consequence (proposed; owner to ratify, and it does not block P1):**

- Until counsel L4 answers questions Q1–Q4 below, `provider: 'golfnow'` T0 links stay in the code (the host
  gate, the allow-list and dry-run tests are unaffected), but **course-native links are primary in anything
  published**. In practice this is the kill branch's shipping posture, reached as a hedge rather than a finding.
- The kill fires in full if L4 reads "in-line links" to cover hyperlinks, **or** if L4 holds that E11 is not the
  incorporated list and no authoritative copy of the §5.1 list is obtained by day 5.

**Questions for counsel L4 (exact clauses quoted above):**

- **Q1.** Does E11 "No Repurposing", "frames or in-line links", reach a plain outbound hyperlink to a public
  facility URL?
- **Q2.** Is E11 (versantmedia.com) the list §5.1 incorporates, given that §5.1's link (E9) is dead? If not, where
  is the operative text?
- **Q3.** Do §2.1 "personal, non-commercial use only" and E11 "No Commercial Use" bind a third party that only
  links, as opposed to a user who accesses the Services?
- **Q4.** Does E11 "No Data Extraction" ("… robots … to access … monitor … any portion of the Versant Services")
  cover a low-volume, scheduled `HEAD`/`GET` liveness check of facility URLs (see the implications below)?

### Implications for P2 site code (interpretation)

- **`scripts/check-links.mjs --live`.** It sends a scheduled `HEAD` (and a `GET` on 405/501) with the UA
  `GolfRaven-LinkChecker/1.0` to each allow-listed booking URL, including `www.golfnow.com` facility pages. That
  fits the literal words of E11 "No Data Extraction": "software robots … to access … monitor … any portion of
  the Versant Services". It may also meet "No Unauthorized Access" ("any technology or means other than those
  provided or authorized"). The check is low-volume and copies no content, which argues against the clause
  applying, but the words are broad. **Recommendation:** until Q4 clears, run `--live` against course-native
  hosts only, and check GolfNow links by hand or not at all. The same question applies to the Addendum G/H X4
  liveness GETs if a runner scripts them. Addendum G's rule that "no tool automates GolfNow search while the X6
  terms read is outstanding" is **not** lifted by this read, because the read found nothing that authorises
  automation.
- **Labelling links "GolfNow".** §4.1 reserves GolfNow's trademarks and trade names, and E11 bars use "in a
  manner that suggests an association with our … brands" without written agreement. *Interpretation:* a
  plain-text label that only names the destination (for example "Book on GolfNow ↗") is the lowest-risk form.
  Avoid the GolfNow logo, "official", "partner" or "powered by" wording, and any styling in GolfNow's trade
  dress. A one-line non-affiliation note is cheap insurance. Whether the text label itself is permissible
  nominative use is a question for counsel, and this memo does not decide it.
- **Unchanged guardrails, now with a clause behind them.** §6.3 "never display GolfNow prices or inventory at T0"
  and "no scraping" rest on §2.1, E11 "No Data Extraction", "No Manipulation" ("… display … frame …") and
  "No Repurposing". Keep them.
- **T1 (P7).** Public pages publish no affiliate economics, scopes or payment flow. SP3 Q1 and Q2 can only be
  answered after a GolfNow account plus approved access, or through the E3 partnership form. Plan A10 remains
  unverified, neither confirmed nor refuted.
