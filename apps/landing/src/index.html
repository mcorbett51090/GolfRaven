Privacy, in brief

GolfRaven is operated by [OPERATING ENTITY NAME — TBD]. Contact [CONTACT EMAIL — TBD] with any privacy question, or to be removed.

  • 16+ only. GolfRaven is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect signups from anyone under 16. The signup form records your confirmation of this alongside your email.
  • What we store. Your email address, your trail preference, when you signed up and when you confirmed, the wording of the consent you agreed to (including this age confirmation), and a short-lived, keyed hash of your IP address (never the raw address) used only for abuse limits, kept for about 3 days. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.
  • How long we keep it. If you never confirm, your signup is deleted automatically after 30 days. If you confirm and later unsubscribe, we keep the record until the K2 signal-test result is recorded, then delete it within 30 days of your unsubscribe.
  • Double opt-in. You're added to the list only after you click the confirmation link we email you.
  • Unsubscribe any time. Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Who processes this data. Cloudflare (hosting, storage, and the Turnstile bot check below) and Resend (delivers the confirmation email). Neither is used for advertising.
  • No trackers, one spam-prevention exception. This page loads no analytics or advertising scripts of any kind. The one third party it loads, only once signups are open, is Cloudflare Turnstile — a challenge that confirms you're not a bot before your signup is accepted. It does not track you across other sites.
